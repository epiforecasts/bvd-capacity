# Terms for the contents of data/

The code in this repository is MIT, in [../LICENSE](../LICENSE). That licence
covers the code and nothing in this directory.

Every table below carries the terms of what it was built from, not one blanket
licence for the directory. Figures extracted from a source are facts and not
copyrightable; the `evidence_quote` column, wherever a table has one, is the
publisher's own sentence, verbatim, and stays under that publisher's terms.

## Extracted from INSP situation reports

`facilities.csv`, `facility_events.csv`, `facility_opening.csv`,
`facility_flags.csv`, `organisations.csv`, `indicators.csv`,
`indicator_appearances.csv`

INSP holds whatever rights attach to its situation reports, and this
repository is not affiliated with INSP. The extraction and the structure of
these tables are this repository's own work, published under
[CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) with attribution to
INSP. The quoted French sentences these rows carry are reproduced from INSP's
published reports.

## Extracted from WHO documents

`capacity_indicators.csv`, `external_corroboration_who_afro.csv`,
`external_corroboration_who_don.csv`

All three tables are read from WHO Disease Outbreak News and WHO AFRO weekly
situation reports, © World Health Organization, licensed
[CC BY-NC-SA 3.0 IGO](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/3.0/igo/).
Non-commercial use and share-alike travel with anything built on this
material, and the `evidence_quote` text in these rows is WHO's own wording,
not this repository's. Each row also carries its own `publisher` and `licence`
column, naming WHO or WHO AFRO as appropriate.

Whether the extracted figures and the quoted sentence together make a row a
derived work of WHO's, rather than the figure being an uncopyrightable fact
and the quote a separately licensed quotation, is genuinely unsettled; this
file treats the whole row as carrying WHO's terms rather than deciding it.
